About

I'm Paing Phyo Khant, from Myanmar and now living in Yokohama. After language school in Tokyo, I started at Nihon Kogakuin College, where I'm studying to become a system engineer. I graduate in March 2027.

What got me into engineering was the inventory system at my part-time job. I watched mistakes drop and the work run more smoothly, and I felt how much a good system can help. Now I want to be the person who builds systems that people can use without getting lost.

Why TOMATO

I want to build systems that support the people who work on the front line, so that anyone can use them without getting lost. That is why I applied to TOMATO. I first became interested in engineering when I used an inventory system at my part-time job and saw how much it improved work efficiency.

At college I'm learning Java, Python and system design, and I'm drawn to developing from the user's point of view. I was strongly attracted that TOMATO builds a wide range of business systems, including web systems and logistics, and cares about training its people and their technical growth.

I will use my ability to read the situation around me and act, and in the future I want to contribute as an engineer who builds systems that users love for a long time.

What I'm good at

My strength is that I look at what's happening around me and act on my own. At my part-time job there were times when we ran the shop with very few people, and at busy times we called out to each other and worked together. When I judged that the register was about to get crowded, I immediately asked another staff member to help there, and I looked at the whole floor, tidying up and preparing the next task. As a result, customers didn't have to wait, even at the busiest times, and the shop ran smoothly.

Hobby: mountain climbing

Mountain climbing is my hobby. The first time I climbed Mt. Takao, I realized I was out of shape. So I made a bigger goal: Mt. Fuji. I ran 5 km every weekend, asked experienced climbers about gear, and planned my route carefully. I made it to the top.
